# Closed beta (KIT-150 to KIT-152)

150 urban and 30 rural households, 8 Feb to 7 Mar 2027 (PLAN.md Phase 7). These are working drafts written from the
BRD and PRD; each says what still needs a person.

| Document | Story | What it is | Still needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| [RECRUITMENT.md](RECRUITMENT.md) | KIT-150 | Who to recruit, how they join, the consent form, the phone loan plan | A lawyer's read of the consent form; a signed NGO/SHG partner; Hindi and regional translations checked by a native speaker |
| [FIELD_PROTOCOL.md](FIELD_PROTOCOL.md) | KIT-151 | How to watch first-time voice use without helping: script, recording rules, what to log | The regional language decision (D4); observers; 5 sessions per dialect booked |
| [GATE_REVIEW.md](GATE_REVIEW.md) | KIT-152 | The launch targets, where each number comes from, and the page to record the decision on | Real data; the decision |

The numbers for the gate come from `private.beta_gate_report` in the database (it covers every household, with or
without the analytics consent) and from the PostHog dashboard in `docs/ANALYTICS.md`. The Monday report in GitHub
Actions prints the gate table every week.
