Privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026. This policy covers the Cookmate app for Android and the Cookmate web app.

In short. Cookmate keeps what it needs to suggest and guide your cooking: your kitchen, your family's food preferences and what you cook. Photos of your fridge and your voice are used for one answer and not stored. There are no ads, and your data is never sold. You can export or delete everything from the app at any time.

Photos and voice

When you photograph your fridge, a shelf, a shop bill or your pan, the photo goes to our AI provider for that one answer and is not stored. When you use Voice Cook, your speech is turned into text to answer you and neither the audio nor the text is stored; we keep only how long the session lasted and what it cost. Voice commands such as "next" use your phone's own speech recognition.

Consent

When you start, Cookmate asks separately for voice processing, remembering your family's tastes, and analytics. None is switched on for you, and you can change each one later in You → Privacy. Notifications are asked for separately by Android.

Your rights

  • See and take your data: You → Privacy → Download my data gives you a file with everything.
  • Correct it: edit people, preferences, pantry and memories in the app, or write to us.
  • Delete it: You → Privacy → Delete my account removes everything at once. Other ways are on the account deletion page.
  • Withdraw consent at any time in You → Privacy. What was done before stays lawful; the feature stops.
  • Nominate someone to act for you if you die or can't act, by writing to us.
  • Complain: write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can go to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

The person who sets up Cookmate must be 18 or older. They can add children to their household so that meals suit them; for a child we keep only a name or nickname, the age group and food needs. Cookmate is not meant to be used by children on their own.

Security

All data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Each household can read only its own data, enforced by the database itself. Access to the servers is limited to the people who run Cookmate. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
