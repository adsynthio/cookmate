# Play Store listing text

Paste each block into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing. English (India) is the default
language; add Hindi and Kannada under "Manage translations → Add your own translations". Limits: app name 30
characters, short description 80, full description 4,000. `python3 tools/play/check_listing.py` checks them.

**Please read the Hindi and Kannada text yourself before pasting.** It was written by Claude, so a native
speaker should check the tone.

Claims rule: say only what the app does today. Voice Cook (talking with the app) is invite-only at launch, so the
listing promises voice commands (next, repeat, timer), which every phone gets.

## English (India), `en-IN`

<!-- name -->
```
Cookmate: Indian Home Cooking
```

<!-- short -->
```
What to cook from what's at home, step by step, in Kannada, Hindi or English.
```

<!-- full -->
```
Cookmate is a cooking app for Indian homes. Tell it what's in your kitchen, and it tells you what to cook tonight and helps you make it, step by step.

WHAT TO COOK, FROM WHAT YOU HAVE
• Tonight's picks from your own pantry, for the people eating today.
• Take a photo of your fridge, a shelf or a shop bill, and Cookmate adds the items for you. Scan barcodes too.
• Missing something? Cookmate swaps the way an experienced cook does: Eno for soda, malai for cream, amchur for lemon.
• A fancy dish is just simple parts. Cookmate shows you the parts you already know.
• "Make it simpler" gives you the easy version of a dish on a busy day.

COOK WITH CONFIDENCE
• Clear steps with what to look for: "onions golden at the edges", "oil leaves the masala".
• Pressure cooker whistles, gas or induction settings, and an air-fryer option for fried dishes.
• Timers that keep running while you cook. Say "next", "repeat" or "timer" with messy hands.
• Something went wrong? Too salty, too spicy, burnt at the bottom: get a fix right away.
• Works offline once a dish is open.

MADE FOR YOUR FAMILY
• Each person's diet: veg, Jain, eggetarian, non-veg, vegan, and the foods they avoid.
• Children and elders in the family change spice and steps.
• Fasting days and festivals: vrat food, Navratri, Diwali, Ugadi and more.
• Cookmate learns your family's likes and your own kitchen tricks, and you can see, edit or delete what it remembers.

LESS WASTE, LESS SPENDING
• Use-soon reminders for things about to spoil, and ideas for leftovers.
• A rough cost per plate, and what a cheaper swap saves.
• A shopping list you can share on WhatsApp in one tap.

COOK TOGETHER
• Private cooking circles for family and friends: share what you cooked, save their dishes. No public feed.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
• Kannada, Hindi, Hinglish and English.

SAFE AND PRIVATE
• AI-written recipes are checked against food-safety rules and your family's diet before you see them, are labelled, and can be reported in one tap.
• Doneness of meat, fish and eggs is always checked by you, never guessed from a photo.
• No ads. No selling of your data. Delete your account and everything in it at any time from the app.

Cookmate gives cooking guidance, not medical or nutrition advice.
```
