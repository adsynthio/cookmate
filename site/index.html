What to cook, from what's at home.

Cookmate is a cooking app for Indian homes. Tell it what's in your kitchen, and it tells you what to cook tonight and helps you make it, step by step, in Kannada, Hindi or English.

  • Tonight's picks from your own pantry, for the people eating today.
  • Take a photo of your fridge or a shop bill, and the items are added for you.
  • Missing something? Swaps the way an experienced cook does: Eno for soda, malai for cream.
  • Each person's diet, fasting days and festivals, with children and elders in mind.
  • No ads. Delete your account and everything in it at any time.

Coming soon to Google Play, for Android phones in India.
