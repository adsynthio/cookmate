Englishहिंदीಕನ್ನಡ

From the app (fastest)

  1. Open Cookmate and go to the You tab.
  2. Tap Privacy, then Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete everything. It happens at once.

Want a copy first? In the same place, use Download my data.

Without the app

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the subject "Delete my Cookmate account" and the phone number you linked in the app. We'll send a code to that number to make sure it's you, then delete the account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

If you never linked a phone number, the account isn't tied to anything we can check, so it can only be deleted from the app on the phone that made it.

What gets deleted

  • Deleted: your account and phone number, and every household you alone own, with everything in it: people, pantry, shopping lists, cooking history, feedback, memories, family recipes, posts in cooking circles, notification tokens and consent settings on the phone.
  • If you share a household with someone who also owns it, you're removed from it and their data stays with them.
  • Kept for a limited time: the record of the consents you gave, for 3 years, because the law asks us to prove consent; and the AI request log (no text, only feature, size and cost) until it expires after 90 days.
