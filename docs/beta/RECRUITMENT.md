# Beta recruitment and onboarding (KIT-150)

Draft. The consent form is not legal advice: have a lawyer who knows the DPDP Act read it before anyone signs.

## Who

| Group | Households | How they are found | Phone |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Urban (launch city) | 150 | Friends-of-team referrals capped at 30; the rest through housing-society and school-parent WhatsApp groups | Their own Android phone, 3 GB RAM or more |
| Rural pilot | 30 | Through the partner NGO or self-help group (SHG), 2 villages, at most 15 households each | Their own where it runs the app; otherwise a loan phone |

Mix to aim for across both groups (it is what the product claims to serve):
- at least 40% cook mainly in Hindi or Hinglish, and the rural group in the regional language once D4 is decided;
- at least 25% of households with a child under 12, and 15% with an elder;
- vegetarian, eggetarian and non-vegetarian homes each present; at least 5 Jain households;
- at least 20 homes where the person who cooks is not the phone's owner.

Not in the beta: anyone under 18 as the account holder; households where the cook says they don't want to take part,
even if the phone's owner does.

## Joining, step by step

1. **Invite.** A short message in their language with the install link and the name of a person to call.
2. **Consent conversation** (in person for rural, by call for urban): read the form aloud, answer questions, then sign
   or record a spoken yes. A copy stays with the household.
3. **Install and onboarding** on their own. Urban households do it alone (that is part of the test). Rural households
   do it during the first field visit, observed under FIELD_PROTOCOL.md.
4. **In-app consent** is separate and stays in the app: voice processing, memory, analytics and notifications are each
   asked, none pre-ticked, each can be switched off later (You → Privacy).
5. **Week 1 check-in call** (10 minutes): did they cook with it, what stopped them.
6. **Leaving** at any time: You → Privacy → Delete my account removes everything at once. Loan phones are collected
   and wiped.

## Consent form (to be read aloud; one page)

> **Cookmate trial: what we ask and what you can expect**
>
> Cookmate is an app that suggests what to cook from what is in your kitchen and guides you while you cook, by touch
> or by voice. It is still being tested. We are asking your household to try it for four weeks.
>
> **What we collect while you use it:** what you tell it is in your kitchen, the dishes you cook with it, your
> ratings and notes, and how you use the app. If you use voice, your speech is sent to our speech and AI providers to
> understand you; we do not keep recordings unless you separately agree in the app.
>
> **What we do not do:** we do not sell your information, show you ads, or share it with anyone outside the team
> building Cookmate and the service providers that run it. We do not read your contacts, messages or photos.
>
> **Your choices:** taking part is voluntary and free. You can stop at any time, without giving a reason, and
> nothing is lost by stopping. You can download everything we hold about your household or delete it, from the app
> (You → Privacy) or by asking the person named below.
>
> **Food safety:** Cookmate gives cooking guidance. You remain in charge of your kitchen; if anything it suggests
> seems unsafe or wrong, don't follow it, and tell us.
>
> **If someone visits your home to watch you use it** (rural pilot): they will only watch and take notes. They will
> ask before recording sound or taking any photo, and you can say no and still take part.
>
> **Loan phone** (if you receive one): it stays ours, you are not charged if it breaks in normal use, and we collect
> and erase it at the end.
>
> **Questions or complaints:** [name, phone number, hours]. Grievance officer: [name, contact].
>
> ☐ I agree to take part. ☐ I agree that a visit may be audio recorded. ☐ I agree that photos of the cooking (not of
> faces) may be taken.
>
> Name / signature or thumbprint / date. Read aloud by: ______ in (language): ______

Fill the bracketed parts before use. Keep signed forms for the retention period your lawyer advises, apart from app
data, and record only a household code (not the name) in field notes.

## Loan phones (rural pilot)

- **How many:** 12 (enough for 30 households if up to 40% need one), plus 2 spares.
- **Which:** an Android phone with 3 GB RAM, the lowest the PRD supports, so the pilot tests the hard case.
- **Set up before handing over:** Cookmate installed and signed in to nothing; a prepaid data SIM with 4 weeks of
  data; screen lock off or a code the household chooses; a printed card with the helpline number.
- **Register:** phone ID ↔ household code, date out, date back, condition. Kept by the field lead, not in the app.
- **Return:** collected in week 5; the household is offered a data export first; then the account is deleted from the
  app and the phone is factory reset.

## Partner NGO / SHG

What to agree in writing before the pilot (the story asks for a signed partner):
- they introduce the households and a local coordinator attends first visits;
- they are paid a fixed amount per household for the coordinator's time, not per result;
- they do not see app data; they do see anonymised findings at the end;
- either side can stop the pilot in a village at any time;
- households are thanked the same way whether they finish the four weeks or not (decide what: a small kitchen item is
  usual; cash can pressure people to stay).
